# Superseded draft (shelved)

This directory holds the earlier draft of the paper, shelved on 2026-09-11 at
the author's request and retained for provenance. It is self-contained: its
`figures/` is a snapshot copy, so `report.tex` still compiles here unchanged.

It was superseded because:

- the hypothesis was a thin empirical scaling question ("does the advantage
  grow with complexity?") with no mechanism attached;
- the narrative read as a verdict against TRM rather than as an investigation,
  and was not fair to the original method's actual claims;
- it offered no account of *why* the recursive trunk underperformed;
- it treated the parameter-count mismatch between the DNN and TRM trunks as a
  limitation rather than as the confound that the mechanistic analysis later
  showed it to be.

The current paper lives one level up (`papers/report.md`, `report.tex`,
`report.pdf`) and supersedes this in full. Data, figures 1-7 and all reported
numbers are unchanged between the two; the framing, the mechanistic analysis
(figures 8-10) and the experimental programme are new.
